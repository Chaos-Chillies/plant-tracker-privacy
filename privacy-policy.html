Plant Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Plant Tracker ("the app") is a plant-care tracker for Android. This policy explains how it handles your information.

No data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts and no sign-in. Everything you enter — plants, schedules, history and notes — is stored only on your own device.

No servers

The app does not send anything to us or to any third party. The QR scanner runs on your phone and reads only the app's own plant labels. Nothing is uploaded.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan plant QR labels on the device.
  • Notifications — used only to show your care reminders.
  • Vibration — a short buzz to confirm a label was scanned.

Backups

Any backup you create from the Settings screen is a file you control and save yourself. We never receive it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
